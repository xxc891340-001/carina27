Rental Preparation 出租准备

Get the basics right to avoid big headaches later 把规则说清楚，减少后期大麻烦

  • ✓ Understand your rights as a resident landlord (lodger / excluded occupier rules) 了解自住房东的权利（lodger / excluded occupier 规则）

  • ✓ Prepare simple house rules for shared spaces (no formal tenancy contract required) 准备简单的公共区域使用规则（不需要正式租赁合同）

Tenant Screening & Pre-Move-In Education 租客筛选与入住前教育

Prevent problems by setting expectations early 提前说清楚，预防90%的纠纷

  • ✓ Ask the right questions about lifestyle, working hours, and habits during screening 筛选时问清楚生活习惯、作息时间
  • ✓ Share clear guidelines on looking after the property before move-in 入住前发给租客爱护房屋的规则说明
  • ✓ Let the lodger know the process will be fair and documented — this alone deters many problems 告知租客整个流程是透明记录的——这本身就有双向震慑作用

3

Move-In Property Condition Record 入住房屋状态记录

★ Most Important Step ★ 最关键步骤

Create strong evidence from day one — your best protection against deposit disputes 第一天就把证据做足——押金纠纷时你最强的保护

  • ✓ Document every room and shared area with photos and detailed descriptions 逐房间拍照 + 详细文字描述
  • ✓ Walk through the record together with your lodger on move-in day 入住当天和租客一起走一遍并确认
  • ✓ Both parties confirm — each keeps a copy of the PDF report 双方确认，各保留一份PDF报告

Move-Out & Deposit Settlement 退租与押金处理

⚠ Safety matters here ⚠ 安全第一

End safely and fairly — especially important if things get tense 安全、公平地结束出租——如有压力务必注意安全

  • ✓ Compare move-out condition with the original move-in record 对比入住记录，逐房间检查状态
  • ✓ Generate a clear difference report to support deposit deduction decisions 生成差异报告，支持押金扣款决定
  • ⚡ If you feel threatened: know when to call 999, how to record safely, and what to say 如遭威胁：何时拨打999、如何录音、以及可以直接说的安全话术
